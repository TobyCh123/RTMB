Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 14 November 2026

This policy covers the RTMB mobile application and the RTMB website. RTMB is operated by Toby Challis, who can be contacted at chalice.tc@gmail.com.

Information we handle

Depending on how you use RTMB, we may handle:

  • Account details such as your account identifier, username, email address (for email accounts), account type, drink preference, and device platform.
  • Drink-tracking entries and related details you choose to provide, such as drink name, type, size, quantity, time, brewery, country, alcohol content, or a venue/location label.
  • Group membership, group activity, and information shown to other members when you use group features. Drink entries and profile names may be visible to people in a group.
  • A push-notification token, which may be generated during account setup to support push notifications, and settings or data stored locally on your device.
  • When you use website forms, the email address and any message or reason you submit.
  • Technical request information, such as IP address, browser or device information, and request times, which may be received by the services that deliver the app or website.

How information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, provide drink tracking and group features, synchronize your data, deliver notifications you enable, respond to requests, maintain and protect the service, and meet legal obligations. Information displayed in group features is shared with the relevant group members as part of those features.

Service providers and external requests

RTMB uses service providers to operate the app and website. When a device or browser connects to a provider, that provider may receive technical information such as the IP address and browser or device request details, in addition to the information needed for the requested feature.

  • Supabase provides app authentication and database services. Account and app data are sent to Supabase to provide the app. Supabase may process connection and security information. See the Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Expo provides app infrastructure, updates, and push-notification services. When push notifications are used, a push token is generated and may be stored with your app account. See the Expo Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process technical request information when pages are accessed. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Formspree processes website waitlist, contact, and account-deletion forms. Information you submit, and technical details associated with the form request, may be processed by Formspree. See Formspree's Privacy Policy.
  • Wikimedia hosts some images displayed on the website. Your browser may contact Wikimedia when those images are loaded. See Wikimedia's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple and Google provide app distribution and platform services. Their handling of data through their operating systems and stores is governed by their own notices.

Website forms, cookies, and analytics

Website forms are optional. If you submit one, the information entered is sent to Formspree and may be forwarded to the RTMB operator so they can respond or process your request. Website hosting and form providers may use necessary technical storage or server logs; check their current policies for details.

Retention and deletion

App account and tracking information is kept while needed to provide the service. You can start account deletion from the app's Settings, or request help at chalice.tc@gmail.com. Website form submissions are retained for as long as needed to respond and maintain appropriate records. Some information may remain for a limited time in provider backups or logs, or where retention is required to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, or protect the service.

International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. The locations and safeguards can depend on provider and account configuration.

Your choices and rights

You may manage notification permissions through your device settings and may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information by contacting chalice.tc@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights, including rights to object to or restrict certain processing or to receive a copy of your information. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

Children and age requirements

The Terms of Service state that users must be at least 18 years old. The app's alcohol-related notice also tells users to meet the legal drinking age where they live. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a person under 18 has provided information, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

Security and changes

Reasonable measures are used to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. We may update this policy by publishing a revised version here and changing the date above. Please review it when it changes.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact chalice.tc@gmail.com.
